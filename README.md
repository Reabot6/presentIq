## What is presentIQ?

Presenting is hard, and for many people it's a real barrier in school and at work. **presentIQ** helps people practise and improve. It watches you, listens to you, and tells you how you did.

It's built with **inclusive education** in mind: giving people who struggle with presenting a private, judgement-free way to build confidence and skills.



---

## Features

```
   ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────┐   ┌───────────┐
   │   QUIZ    │ → │  RECORD   │ → │  ANALYSE  │ → │  FEEDBACK │
   └───────────┘   └───────────┘   └───────────┘   └───────────┘
```

- **Pre-presentation quiz**: checks you know your material before you start
- **Computer vision**: watches your delivery to see how well you're doing
- **Gesture marking**: identifies and scores your gestures and body language
- **Speech analysis**: transcribes and analyses how you speak using Whisper
- **Word quality**: assesses how good your word choice and wording are
- **Feedback**: shows you where you did well and what to improve
